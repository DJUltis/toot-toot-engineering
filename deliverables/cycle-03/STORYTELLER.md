# STORYTELLER (cycle-03)

## Narrative focus for cycle-03
CompanionID earns trust by showing its wiring. Cycle-03 makes the device explicit: the pins have names, the radio has a rhythm, the tests prove stability, and the prompts are bounded by safety. The companion becomes a tool you can understand without losing its warmth.

## Story beats to guide technical decisions
- **Clarity:** Pin maps and schematic notes should read like a map, not a riddle.
- **Integrity:** Test plans are part of the story; they prove the companion’s calm.
- **Practicality:** Installation steps reduce friction and invite building.
- **Boundaries:** Prompt fuzzing is a guardrail, not a constraint.

## UX tone anchors
- Provide safe-mode as a comforting reset, not a punishment.
- Keep provisioning steps linear and explicit.
