# STORYTELLER (cycle-02)

## Narrative focus for cycle-02
CompanionID becomes trustworthy by being legible: its signals are predictable, its power use is understandable, and its interactions are gentle but explicit. Cycle-02 turns that into concrete mechanics: the timing of its radio heartbeat, the ritual of pairing, the graceful fallback when power or connectivity is scarce, and the calm resilience of a device that can be reset without losing its identity.

## Story beats to guide technical decisions
- **Rhythm:** Radio arbitration should feel like a steady pulse, not a scramble.
- **Ceremony:** Pairing is a small ritual, with clear confirmation and an easy exit.
- **Resilience:** Safe-mode is a quiet recovery, not a harsh failure.
- **Growth:** Prompt evolution is reversible; the device never feels unstable.
- **Trust:** Power budgets and hardware choices reinforce durability and longevity.

## UX tone anchors
- Use slow, deliberate feedback patterns in pairing and safe-mode.
- Keep BLE provisioning minimal, with a visible "done" state.
- Avoid chatty idle behaviors; the companion should remain calm and predictable.
