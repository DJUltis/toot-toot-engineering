# STORYTELLER (cycle-04)

## Narrative focus for cycle-04
CompanionID becomes buildable. Cycle-04 anchors the project in practical proof: pins are verified, builds can start, and tests show that the device’s calm rhythm survives real-world interruptions.

## Story beats to guide technical decisions
- **Verification:** Pin maps are earned through confirmation, not assumption.
- **Beginner-friendly:** PlatformIO setup should be short, clear, and reliable.
- **Resilience:** BLE disconnect tests prove the companion doesn’t panic.
- **Measurement:** Arbiter timing should be observable, not guessed.
